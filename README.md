# Anex-Sales-Dashoboard
Sales perfomance analytics using powerbi

Project Overview

An interactive Sales Performance Analytics Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze business sales data and support data-driven decision-making.

Business Problems Addressed

- Which regions generate the highest revenue?
- What are the monthly revenue trends?
- Who are the top-performing sales representatives?
- Which sales representatives are underperforming?
- How does sales performance compare between 2024 and 2025?

Key Features

- KPI tracking: Transactions, Quantity, Revenue and Year Change.
- Dynamic year filtering (2024, 2025 and All).
- Monthly revenue trend analysis.
- Regional revenue comparison.
- Top 3 sales representative analysis.
- Bottom 3 sales representative analysis.

Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Data Visualization
- Business Intelligence

Business Value

The dashboard helps identify sales trends, evaluate regional performance, compare sales representatives and highlight opportunities for business improvement.
